Why we're here

BW Packaging on this trip

BW Packaging is Barry-Wehmiller's packaging-machinery business — filling, closing, labeling, end-of-line and fully integrated lines, with a global footprint of roughly 25 manufacturing locations across five continents. Three of its European homes sit right on this itinerary.

Hema — Quimper

📍 Quimper, Brittany · now BW Filling & Closing · Fri Jul 25

BWFC🏢 30,666 sq ft🔑 Owned
  • ◆ Founded in Quimper in 1936 — one of BW's oldest European roots.
  • ◆ Invented the first rotary piston filler for sauces & jam in 1939.
  • ◆ Fillers for liquid, viscous & semi-solid products — oil, dairy, dressings, meat & fish.
  • ◆ 3,500+ machines across 100 countries; joined BW Filling & Closing in 2024.
